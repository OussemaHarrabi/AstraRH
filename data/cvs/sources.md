# CV corpus sources

- **Dataset:** `opensporks/resumes` on Hugging Face — 2,484 resume examples, CC0-1.0.
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/opensporks/resumes
- **Original:** Kaggle `snehaanbhawal/resume-dataset` (CC0-1.0), scraped from LiveCareer resume examples.
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/snehaanbhawal/resume-dataset
- **Selection:** 30 technical resumes from Information Technology, Engineering, Designer and Digital Media categories.
- **Transformation:** source text is sanitized (emails, phones, URLs, company placeholders, locations, clearance/military references), paired with fictional identities and regenerated in PDF/DOCX/HTML/Markdown/TXT/JSON.
- **No real person is represented by the final files.** Source text may contain residual template artifacts; report them before redistribution.
- **Generated:** 2026-10-31.
