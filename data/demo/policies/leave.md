# Annual leave
Employees earn 22 working days of paid annual leave per year. After 3 years of service, this rises to 25 working days.

# Carrying leave over
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and must be used by 31 March.

# Requesting leave
Leave requests go to your manager at least 5 working days in advance. Requests of more than 2 weeks need 1 month's notice.

# Sick leave
Employees receive 15 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is needed for absences of more than 2 days.

# Parental leave
Mothers receive 14 weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave. Both apply from the first day of employment.
