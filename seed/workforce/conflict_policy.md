# Carthage Demo Systems — Conflict Resolution Policy (fictional)

*Reference document for the workforce conflict-management agent. Fictional, for the AstraRH demo only. The agent may cite sections by their number (e.g. §4.2) when suggesting a process. The agent suggests process only; a human mediator decides every outcome.*

## 1. Scope and principles

1.1 This policy covers workplace conflicts between colleagues: workload and task-distribution disputes, communication and process friction, and scheduling or coverage fairness.

1.2 Principles: resolve at the lowest level possible, keep every case confidential, treat all parties fairly and without blame, and base any decision on facts, never on assumptions about a person.

1.3 This policy does **not** apply to misconduct, harassment or safety issues. Those follow the separate Code of Conduct and are escalated immediately to HR leadership (§7).

## 2. Confidentiality

2.1 A case is visible only to its named parties and the assigned mediator.

2.2 Case content is never shared with other teams, never used in performance reviews, and never entered into any analytics or graph system.

2.3 Records are kept only as long as needed to resolve the case and confirm the outcome.

## 3. Roles

3.1 **Party** — anyone named in the case.

3.2 **Mediator** — a neutral HR business partner (or a manager not involved in the dispute) who runs the process and records the outcome.

3.3 **The agent** — prepares a neutral structured summary and suggests a process with citations. It never decides, never contacts the parties on its own, and never assigns fault.

## 4. The standard process

4.1 **Intake.** The party describes the situation in their own words. The agent produces a neutral, factual summary and classifies the case type.

4.2 **Suggested process.** The agent proposes next steps drawn from this policy, with citations, for the mediator to approve or change.

4.3 **Informal resolution first.** For workload, communication and scheduling cases, a facilitated conversation between the parties is attempted before any formal step.

4.4 **Facilitated meeting.** The mediator meets the parties together, agrees concrete changes, and sets a review date.

4.5 **Resolution record.** The mediator records what was agreed and the review date. Closing a case is a formal, logged action.

## 5. Timelines

5.1 Acknowledge a new case within 2 working days.

5.2 Hold the first facilitated meeting within 10 working days.

5.3 Review the agreed outcome within 1 month of resolution.

## 6. Case-type guidance

6.1 **Workload / task distribution.** Review the facts of how work was assigned over a recent period. Agree a fairer distribution rule going forward. Do not single out any individual as the cause.

6.2 **Communication / process.** Clarify the process and deadlines in dispute. Agree a clear step with owners and timing so expectations are shared.

6.3 **Scheduling / coverage.** Review the rotation data. Rebalance using a fixed, transparent rotation shared with the whole team.

## 7. Escalation

7.1 If informal resolution fails, or if the case involves possible misconduct, harassment or safety, escalate to HR leadership at once.

7.2 Escalation and case closure both require a formal human decision.
